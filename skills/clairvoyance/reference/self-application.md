# The sweep, run on this skill

This is a worked record rather than an illustration. The skill was drafted,
then swept with its own method at `campaign` size — all seven directions, once
— and the draft was changed by what came back. Every edit named below is in the
shipped file. It is kept for three reasons: it is the only honest way to claim
the method does anything, it demonstrates the routing table on findings that
were not chosen to be convenient, and it shows what a real yield looks like
so that a run producing seven findings knows to be suspicious of itself.

**The frame the draft inherited:** the request arrived as *make a flagship skill
about seeing out of the box*. Three commitments came with it — that the
deliverable is a document, that "flagship" means large, and that more sight is
the goal. The first two survived the sweep. The third did not.

---

## Behind — what is this request a solution to?

**Found:** every skill in the library points inward at executing the request
correctly. Nothing in it can notice that the request
was wrong. So the outcome behind *make a skill about creativity* is narrower and
sharper than the words: give the library an organ for noticing.

That reading has a consequence the draft was getting wrong. If the goal is
*the library gains this capacity*, then the scarce thing is not prose — it is
**reachability**. A flagship skill nobody routes to is a well-written file, and
the draft was spending its effort on the document while the thing that decides
whether it ever runs is the description, the spine, and the inbound
cross-references.

**Routed:** to the gate. Remaining effort was re-weighted toward the
orchestrator seat in all seven tracks and the inbound edges from the six skills
whose failure modes hand off here, rather than toward more sections.

**Also found:** the draft was over-defended. It stated the null rule three
times and had more lines on restraint than on method — which is what a skill
written by someone worried about being annoying looks like. One restatement cut.

---

## Under — what does my own vocabulary assume?

**Found, and this was the sweep's best result:** the draft said *the frame*,
singular, throughout — after listing four separate sources it came from. That
is precisely the failure this direction names, committed in the text that names
it. A request does not have one frame. It has several, stacked, from different
places, and **they can disagree with each other.**

Which turned out to be worth more than the correction. A disagreement between
two frames is the cheapest sight available anywhere, because the noticing has
already happened without you: you did not step outside the frame, two frames
stepped outside each other. The reflex on meeting one — pick the more
authoritative source, move on — discards the most valuable signal in the run.

**Routed:** to the gate. New subsection, *Two frames disagreeing is the cheapest
sight there is*. It is now the most useful paragraph in the file and nothing in
the draft would have produced it.

---

## Above — what class is this an instance of?

**Found:** the class is *an agent inherits a frame and has no organ for noticing*,
and it is wider than the request. Frames also arrive from a subagent's returned
conclusion, a tool's output, a search that came back empty, and a memory written
by a session nobody remembers. Each of those arrives as a **finding with its
framing compiled out** — the same shape as a request, and trusted more, because
it looks like evidence.

**Routed:** to the gate. A fifth source added to *Where the frame comes from*.

**Discarded, with the reason:** the general case would be a skill called
something like *examining inherited frames*, which is more accurate and worse.
The name was chosen by the person asking, "clairvoyance" is what someone reaches
for by hand at the moment they need it, and accuracy that costs invocability is
a bad trade in a library whose measured problem is skills not firing.

---

## Beside — what stands next to this once it exists?

**Found:** the discard pile and `tending-tbaguette`'s candidate queue are the
same object seen twice. An observation discarded here because it is not about
this project is, by construction, a lesson that generalizes past this project.

**Found:** `managing-scope-drift` now has a sibling that could be misread as
licensing exactly what it forbids. Resolving that tension in this file only is
half a fix — the reader who arrives from the other direction never sees it.

**Routed:** both to the gate. A line in the routing table pointing discards at
the candidate queue, and a reciprocal edge added to `managing-scope-drift`
saying that noticing is not drifting and naming where noticing goes.

---

## Ahead — what does this commit its readers to?

**Found:** the second-order cost of a skill about seeing more is that it becomes
the most sophisticated available excuse for not finishing. *I looked into it and
the request was the wrong shape* is indistinguishable from insight until someone
asks where the deliverable is, and this skill would be the thing that licensed it.

**Routed:** to the gate, as a rule that outranks the routing table itself — a
sweep is never a reason something did not ship, and a reframe raised and
declined leaves the original ledger fully owed.

**Also found and discarded:** naming seven directions commits the file to that
taxonomy. Checked and it is not a one-way door — an eighth would append. No
action.

---

## Against — what if the answer is the opposite?

**Found:** almost nothing, and the direction is recorded anyway because that is
the point of recording it. Two inversions were tried honestly. *Cut it to one
page* — rejected: without the tells the directions are a creativity listicle,
and the tells are most of the length. *Do not build it* — rejected on the
neighbour check done at design time, which found that
`steelmanning-alternatives` and `red-teaming-your-own-work` both operate on a
candidate that already exists, and neither has a move for the request itself
being the wrong question.

**Routed:** discarded, with reasons. The only yield was a trim.

This is what six of every seven directions look like, and a sweep whose every
direction produces a finding has stopped measuring and started performing.

---

## Outside — who else has solved this?

**Found:** intelligence analysis has the load-bearing import, and it is the
discipline of asking not *which hypothesis is best supported* but *what
observation would come out differently depending on which is true* — on the
grounds that evidence consistent with every hypothesis is evidence for none.

The draft had no equivalent, and its absence was the difference between a
technique and a sensibility. Without it, competing readings of a request get
argued rather than tested, which is how a sweep turns into an opinion nobody
can check.

**Routed:** to the gate. It became the fifth move, and it is what closes the
loop from seeing to knowing.

**Discarded:** aviation's instrument-versus-visual framing was already carrying
the absence section and a second borrowing would have been decoration. TRIZ's
contradiction was already in the draft. Medicine's differential diagnosis is the
absence enumeration under another name.

---

## The tally

Seven directions, six with a finding, one with a trim. Five findings routed
back to the gate and changed the shipped file; four discarded with reasons
recorded above; one — the reachability re-weighting — changed how the rest of
the work was spent rather than changing the document.

The two that mattered most, *frames disagreeing* and *the discriminating
observation*, arrived through the two directions most likely to be skipped:
the one that examines your own vocabulary, and the one that looks like
decoration.
